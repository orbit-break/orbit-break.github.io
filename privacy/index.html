Your privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

OrbitBreak is an arcade game for iOS and Android. This policy explains this website and the current version of the game. In this policy, we, us, and our refer to Samuel Neumann, the developer of OrbitBreak.

Changes to this policy will appear on this page with an updated date. Where required by applicable law, we will provide additional notice or request consent before introducing changes to how we use personal information.

This website

The OrbitBreak website does not add analytics, advertising, tracking cookies, or browser storage. Images, styles, and scripts are served with the site; we do not load third-party fonts or embedded media.

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub processes connection information to serve and secure the site, including visitors’ IP addresses. See GitHub’s explanation of Pages hosting and GitHub’s Privacy Statement for its practices and retention information.

Messages you choose to send

The contact form prepares an email draft on your device. Filling it out does not submit information to us. When you send the email, we receive your email address and the information you include, such as your name, device details, bug report, or feature suggestion. Your email provider and our email provider also process that correspondence under their own policies.

We use messages to respond, investigate bugs, consider suggestions, and handle privacy requests. We retain support correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary to resolve your request, investigate related bugs, handle follow-up questions or implementation, or meet legal obligations. We delete or anonymize personal information when it is no longer needed for these purposes. You can ask us to delete your correspondence using the contact details below; we may need to retain information to meet legal obligations. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information.

Game progress and preferences

OrbitBreak stores your scores, best distance, tutorial progress, selected ship, and preferences locally on your device using Unity’s local storage. The game does not provide an OrbitBreak account or a custom service for uploading your gameplay profile. Device backup services may include local app data according to your device settings. Removing app data or uninstalling the app may remove local progress; backups can retain copies.

You may delete your local scores and best distance using Options → Reset Records. Deleting local game data in this way does not delete any data already processed by advertising providers.

Advertising

OrbitBreak integrates Google AdMob for rewarded advertisements that can provide a Continue. Watching an ad is optional. Once the consent system permits ad requests, the advertising SDK can initialize and preload ads before you choose to watch one, so SDK data processing is not limited to the moment an ad is watched.

Google’s Mobile Ads SDK may process IP addresses and approximate location, device or advertising identifiers, ad and app interactions, and diagnostics for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention. The information processed depends on the platform, SDK version, device settings, and consent choices. See Google’s Android SDK disclosure, iOS SDK disclosure, and Privacy Policy.

The game uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request consent where required and provides Ad Privacy Options in Options when required by that service. The current game asks whether you are 18 or older and stores that choice locally; it does not ask for your date of birth. Under-18 and undisclosed-age selections use the SDK’s child and under-age protections. These settings do not mean that no technical data is processed. Age selection and Ad Privacy Options can be changed from Options.

We currently use Google AdMob to provide rewarded advertising. We may add other advertising providers in future versions. When we do, we will update this policy to describe their data practices and update privacy notices and consent choices where required.

Advertising privacy choices

You can review or withdraw advertising consent through Options → Ad Privacy Options, when that option is available. Google’s User Messaging Platform manages these choices and stores consent information on your device. OrbitBreak does not maintain a separate consent record.

Withdrawing consent does not necessarily disable advertising. Depending on your choices and Google’s advertising configuration, non-personalized or limited ads may remain available, or ads may become unavailable. These ads may still involve technical data processing as described throughout this document.

You can change your age-group selection in Options → Select Age. OrbitBreak stores only whether you selected 18 or older. Under-18 and undisclosed-age selections receive the same protective treatment. Confirming a new selection replaces the previous saved value; the game does not maintain a history of your selections.

Advertising SDKs may preload ads. When you open ad privacy options or confirm an age-group selection, OrbitBreak discards cached ads. Further ad requests use the updated settings and occur only when the consent system permits them.

Diagnostics

Unity Analytics and Unity engine diagnostic data collection are disabled in OrbitBreak’s release configuration.

Google’s advertising SDK may process diagnostic information as described in the Advertising section.

Security

We restrict access to our support mailbox to developers who need it to respond to messages and handle support or privacy requests. Email is processed by our email provider and is not guaranteed to be secure. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information.

Local game data, including high scores, preferences, and your age-group selection, is stored on your device without additional encryption by OrbitBreak. Your device may provide its own security protections.

Intended audience

OrbitBreak is intended for players of all ages. We ask whether you are 18 or older so we can apply appropriate advertising protections. For players who select under 18 or prefer not to disclose their age group, we configure Google AdMob to use child-directed treatment and a G-rated maximum ad content rating.

Optional rewarded ads may be available for any age group, subject to your privacy choices and the advertising SDK’s eligibility checks. These protections do not mean that no technical data is processed.

Parents or guardians can contact us using the details below with questions about their child’s privacy.

Your choices and requests

You can choose not to send a support email, manage app data and advertising identifiers in your device settings, and use the game’s available privacy options. For privacy questions or concerns, contact us using the details below. You can also request access, correction, or deletion of personal information you have sent us.

Requests about data held independently by GitHub, Google, your email provider, or other service providers should also be directed to that provider through its privacy controls.

Contact

Email orbitbreakgame@gmail.com or use the contact page. Include “Privacy request” in the subject so we can identify your request.
