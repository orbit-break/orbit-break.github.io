A field guide to the NeonObservatory

Every world.
A new way to escape.

Some planets give you a moment to think. Others demand a leap of faith. Get to know the worlds that shape your journey, one orbit at a time.

Normal planet

Find your rhythm.

A steady, predictable orbit. Line up your next destination and choose your moment to launch. Just remember: the destruction wave is still moving.

Overheating planet

Your orbit has a deadline.

The countdown starts when you arrive. Launch before this unstable world explodes, or your escape ends here.

Reverse planet

Rethink your timing.

This world sends you orbiting in the opposite direction. Read the new rhythm and line up your launch from a different angle.

Boost planet

Make your momentum count.

Leave this world with extra launch speed. The same one-touch timing sends you further into the next stretch of space.

Dust planet

Keep your ship in mind.

A drifting dust band briefly obscures your ship and hides the trajectory preview. Track your orbit and be ready when the dust clears.

Ice planet

A slower orbit. The same urgency.

Frozen worlds slow your orbit, giving each approach a different rhythm. Your launch speed stays the same, and the destruction wave won’t slow down with you.

Pulsar planet

Catch the pulse.

Its capture field expands and contracts while your orbit stays steady. Watch the changing ring as you plan your approach.

Crumbling planet

There’s no coming back.

Safe to orbit, but only until you leave. Your first launch breaks this world apart and closes its capture field for the rest of the run.

Satellite planet

Mind the moons.

One to three moons circle outside your ship’s orbit. Time your departure to slip past them: a collision in flight ends the journey.

Binary planet

Two worlds. One close encounter.

Two nearby planets each have their own capture field. Choose your line carefully: the nearer world can catch a jump meant for its neighbour.

New orbit. New instinct. Same small ship.
